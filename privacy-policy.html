PaperPop.io – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game PaperPop.io (package name com.paperpop.io, the “Game”), published by Anjani Innovations (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use the Game on Android. By installing or using the Game, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no user accounts and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress (coins, gems, levels, heroes, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive this data.
  • The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue playing, the Game stores the following on your device using the app’s local storage: coins and gems balance, current level and scores, unlocked and equipped heroes, chests, daily reward and mission progress, redeemed promo codes, tutorial status and your settings (sound, music, vibration, notifications, graphics quality).

This data never leaves your device through us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear the Game’s app data in Android Settings. Because it is stored only on your device, we cannot recover or transfer it for you.

Some optional features (for example, opening certain chests) let you choose to watch a rewarded video ad. Watching these ads is always your choice. The reward is given only after Google AdMob confirms the ad was watched.

4. Personalised ads and your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID: on most Android devices go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose “Reset advertising ID” or “Delete advertising ID”.
  • Limit personalised ads: you can manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center.
  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: where required by law, ads are served only after your consent has been requested through Google’s consent message, and you can change your choice at any time. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • Stop all data collection by the Game: uninstall the Game.

5. Permissions used by the Game

The Game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, SMS or precise location.

6. Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the Game, please contact us at the address below and we will help resolve the issue.

7. In-game purchases

The Game does not currently process real-money payments. Coins and gems are virtual items earned in the Game and have no real-world value. If paid features are added in the future, payments will be handled by Google Play, and this policy will be updated.

8. Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the Game. Data collected by Google AdMob and other Google services is kept according to Google’s own retention policies. Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store personal information on our own servers, most requests about advertising data should be made through Google’s privacy tools. You can also contact us and we will help where we can.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add new features. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
